## Description (English)

💥 Your own lab, a crowd of crash-test dummies and a wall full of toys. Make them fly!

🧪 WHAT YOU DO
• Pop, launch and fling dummies with 60+ toys: blasters, bats, rockets, black holes, freeze spells and more
• Grab a dummy, swing it round and throw it into the lava pit or at the cannon's bullseye
• Go limp yourself with RAGDOLL and tumble down the tower
• Unlock new toys as you play, and open free toy eggs
• Build your own contraptions in SANDBOX and share them with a code
• Play the arena games with everyone: Red Light Green Light and Shoot 'Em
• Invite your friends into your lab, or meet them in the plaza

🎮 PLAYS ON
Computer, phone (upright or sideways), tablet and controller (Xbox / PlayStation).

⭐ Favorite the game so you can find your lab again. New toys arrive with every update!

Cartoon effects only: dummies show stars and sparks, no blood.
