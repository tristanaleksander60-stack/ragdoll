# Ragdoll Havoc creator kit (phase 3)

For YouTubers, TikTokers and the owner's own clips. Everything listed works in build 0.19.0.

## Three TikTok formats (7-15 s, upright 9:16, phone held upright)
1. **X-ray bone crack.** Hit a dummy hard (Rocket, Big Popper, the cannon). The X-RAY REPLAY plays by
   itself the first time, later from the Replay button. Press **CLIP IT** to record it. Hook text:
   "how many bones did that break?" End on the score pop ("MEGA CRACK! +412").
2. **Daily experiment fail.** Film today's DAILY EXPERIMENT card (same on every server), try it,
   fail it once, then do it. Caption: "can you do today's?" Post the same day.
3. **Friend launch prank.** Two players in one lab: one grabs the other in the plaza (X on a pad) or
   sends them up the CHIMNEY (December). The PRANK PARTY card shows at the top.

Tips: no music needed (the hits are the sound); first frame = the body in the air; keep the HUD
small (Settings → UI size SMALL); Streamer Mode in CLIPS & LIVE hides the shop cards.

## SHARE CODES
Every X-ray replay shows a 6-letter SHARE CODE (toy + score). Put it in the caption: "beat my
code K7MQ2X". Viewers type it in EXPERIMENTS → TRY IT.

## Codes page (like milestones)
The owner turns on a code at 100, 500 and 1,000 likes (`Config.LIKE_CODES`). Codes are for
everyone; they are never a reward for liking (Roblox rules). Announce them in the game's
description and the group.

## YouTuber guide (what to show in a 10-minute video)
- 0:00 the lab, the dummies, one big hit with the X-ray replay
- 1:00 the WEAPONS shelf: Popper → Rattler → Rocket Tube → Golden Popper (600 takedowns)
- 3:00 the lab's traps: lava, spikes, the wrecking ball, the cannon onto the BULLSEYE
- 5:00 Daily Experiments and the BONE JOURNAL; BOLTS buy the next weapon
- 7:00 Prank Party with a friend; the Christmas lab in December (chimney, present, sleigh ramp)
- 9:00 the arena games (Red Light Green Light, Shoot 'Em) and SANDBOX builds with share codes

## Update rhythm
Updates go out on **Thursday and Saturday** (`Config.UPDATE_DAYS`). On an update day: bump
`Config.Version`, add the top entry to `Shop.WHATS_NEW` (T66 checks they match), and set the title to
`Config.UPDATE_TITLE` ("[UPD] Ragdoll Havoc 💥") for that week. Each update adds one toy, one
experiment set and one journal page (plan, phase 3).
