# The 5 best ads (ChatGPT image prompts), 26 Nov 2026
**How to use:**
1. Open ONE new ChatGPT chat.
2. Attach 3 real screenshots from the game now: a grey dummy close up, the lab with the steel tower, and your avatar holding a gun.
3. Send the SETUP message first.
4. Then send the 5 ads one at a time. Generate each 2-3 times and keep the best.
5. Shrink every image to phone size: you must understand it in 1 second.

**Honesty rule:** only run an ad for something that is live in the game. The dog ad runs only once the German Shepherd is live. Ads that promise things players do not find bring dislikes.

## SETUP (send first)
```
You are a top Roblox ad designer. I will ask for 5 ad images for my Roblox physics ragdoll game "Ragdoll Anatomy". The attached screenshots are the real game: use them as the style and world reference so every ad looks like the real game (grey crash-test ragdoll dummies with simple faces, a sunny lab park with grass, light concrete walkways, a tall grey steel scaffold tower with a long slide, Roblox-style blocky player avatars).

Rules for EVERY image:
- Polished, glossy 3D render in the style of the top Roblox ads and thumbnails: bright saturated colors, strong contrast, a sunny sky, clean shapes, thick dark outlines on the main subject, a strong rim light, a slight wide-angle lens so the action feels close.
- ONE clear main action, readable in 1 second at phone size. The main subject fills at least 60% of the frame.
- Ragdolls are grey plastic crash-test dummies. Any "blood" is cartoon purple paint splats only. NO red blood, NO gore, NO body parts coming off.
- No logos, no Roblox logo, no real brands, no other games' characters, no watermarks, no "free Robux".
- Text only when I ask for it: huge bold rounded letters, white with a thick black outline and a slight 3D drop shadow, at most 3 words.
- Make each ad square 1:1 (1024x1024). After it, I may ask for a 16:9 version.
Reply only with the image.
```

## Run them (USD 500)
1. **Days 1-2:** the 5 ads (4 if the dog is not live) as separate ads, same targeting, about USD 20 each per day.
2. **Pick the winners on both numbers:**
   - CTR;
   - the play time of those players (Creator Hub analytics by source).

   A high CTR with short play time is a bad ad.
3. **Day 3 on:** most of the budget on the top 2. Keep about 10-15 % testing one new variant of the winner.
4. **Change the game page thumbnail to the winning look too.** Roblox's free thumbnail A/B test is in the Creator Hub.
