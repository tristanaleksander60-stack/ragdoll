The ad tile on Roblox is mostly the **icon**, so the icon matters most.

## How to use them (owner)
1. **Open ChatGPT** and start a new chat.
2. **Attach 2-3 real screenshots from the game:**
   - one with a grey dummy up close;
   - one of the lab with the tower;
   - one of your avatar.

   This makes the picture look like the real game. If an ad promises something the game does not show, players leave and dislike.
3. **Paste the MASTER ICON prompt.** Generate 4 times, and keep the best 1-2.
4. **Do the same with icon variants B, C and D**, then the thumbnail prompts.
5. **Check every image at phone size** (shrink it to about 64 px). You must still see what it is in 1 second.
6. **Never use:**
   - blood or gore;
   - the Roblox logo;
   - other games' characters;
   - real brands;
   - "FREE ROBUX";
   - anything not in the game.

   These get rejected by Roblox moderation or by the ads review, and they raise dislikes.
