# MILD EXTRAS: what our rating allows that we do not use yet (cloud research, 27 Nov late)
> The owner: "Undersøg hvad vi ellers må med vores nye rating som vi ikke har gjort endnu som ville være fedt som vi misser ud på"

## Our rating
Roblox content maturity **Mild**, sent 27 Nov with:
- Violence: repeated, mild;
- Blood: light, unrealistic;
- Fear: occasional, mild.

**Mild keeps the game open to Roblox Kids (5-8) and Select (9-15)**, our whole audience. Moderate would cut both younger groups, so we must STAY Mild.

## What Mild allows (Roblox Creator Hub, Help Center)
- **Violence:** repeated mild violence, with unrealistic consequences. We use it.
- **Blood: HEAVY blood, as long as it is unrealistic** (an odd colour or a cartoon shape). Purple counts. We answered only "light", so **we may show much more purple blood and stay Mild.**
- **Fear: REPEATED mild fear:** jump scares, creepy NPCs, ominous music, suspense. We answered "occasional", so **repeated is allowed.**
- **Crude humour (mild):** burping, farting, and cartoon (unrealistic) vomit or poop, such as a poop coil or the poop emoji. **We use NONE of it today, and it is the most viral thing for 9-12-year-olds.**
- **Language:** none (keep it that way).
- **NOT allowed at Mild:**
  - social hangouts (a game made mainly for hanging out and chatting): our multiplayer place must stay a GAME with things to do, never sold as a hangout;
  - realistic blood or gore;
  - romance, gambling and strong language (those are Moderate or Restricted).
- **Always:** "realistic or excessive" violence or crude humour can be moderated whatever the label, so everything stays cartoony and silly, never gross.

## The ideas (each behind its own Config switch; the questionnaire is updated BEFORE the switch goes live)
### A. Crude humour pack (the big one)
1. **Whoopee cushion prop:** put it on a bench; a ragdoll sits, PFFRRT, it jumps up embarrassed, red face, the watchers laugh.
2. **The fart gun (a toy weapon):** a green cartoon cloud; ragdolls in it cough, hold their noses, run, and faint comically.
3. **Burps:** a ragdoll that eats at the picnic grill burps a big cartoon burp (a sound and a little ring of air).
4. **The spin sick:** after the gyro or the washer, a dizzy ragdoll gets a green cartoon face and does a cartoon "blegh" (a green splat shaped like a cartoon puddle, no realism), then wobbles off.
5. **Fart boost:** the go-kart's BOOST or a jetpack toy with a fart sound and a green puff.
6. **The poop-emoji prop:** a smiling cartoon poop that ragdolls step in and slip on (a banana-peel gag).

**Questionnaire:** Crude humour → Mild.

### B. More purple blood (cartoon, heavy)
1. Bigger splash bursts on big hits (a cartoon splat shape, not a spray).
2. Purple splats on walls and floors next to a hit, fading after 20 s.
3. Purple footprints from a ragdoll that walks through a pool.
4. A cartoon purple "fountain" burst on the final KO in the boxing ring.

**Questionnaire:** Blood → heavy, unrealistic (still Mild). Keep every effect cartoony; no red, no realism.

### C. Mild fear (repeated)
1. **A night event, the "Lights Out":** the lab goes dark for 60 s, a flashlight each, the ragdolls whisper and hide, and one jump scare at the end (a ragdoll pops up and screams, then laughs).
2. **A spooky basement or haunted shed:** creepy mannequins that turn their heads when you are not looking, and ominous music.
3. **Jump-scare toy:** a "BOO" mask; put it on and sneak up on a ragdoll: it jumps out of its skin, screams and runs (a big viral moment).
4. **Halloween and Christmas events:** a ghost ragdoll and a pumpkin cannon (seasonal; Christmas already exists).

**Questionnaire:** Fear → repeated, mild.
